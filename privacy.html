Privacy policy

Easyleads Portal, operated by EDGE Marketing · Last updated November 2, 2026

Easyleads Portal is the dashboard EDGE Marketing provides to its clients. It shows each business its own leads, calls and website results. This page explains what information the portal handles and how.

Who can see a dashboard

Each dashboard belongs to one business. Only the people that business and EDGE Marketing give a login to can open it. Logins are created by EDGE Marketing; there is no public sign-up.

What the portal shows

  • Leads from the business's website. When the business adds the Easyleads snippet to its website, what a visitor types into a contact form (never passwords or payment fields) and taps on a phone number are recorded for that business, with the page and the kind of source the visitor came from (for example search, an ad or an AI assistant).
  • The business's Easyleads (CRM) account, when the business has one: contacts, calls, conversations, appointments and pipeline, read with the business's permission.
  • Google Analytics and Google Search Console, when the business gives EDGE Marketing read access to its property: visits, pages, traffic sources, searches, clicks and positions.
  • Marks and activity by the business's own team: who marked a lead as done, pending or spam, and when each login was last active.

Google user data

The portal reads Google Analytics and Google Search Console data only for properties a business has explicitly shared with EDGE Marketing, and only to display that business's own figures on its own dashboard. It requests read-only access. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone other than the business it belongs to, and not used to train AI models. The portal's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where information is kept

Information is kept in EDGE Marketing's Google Workspace (Google Apps Script and Google Sheets) and is sent only to the dashboards of the business it belongs to. Short-lived copies are kept in each viewer's browser to make the dashboard load faster.

Removing information

A business can ask EDGE Marketing at any time to remove a login, disconnect a data source or delete its records. Disconnecting Google Analytics or Search Console stops all further reads immediately; a business can also remove EDGE Marketing's access from the property itself.
